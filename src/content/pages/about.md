I am **Ufkun Karaman**, a **Senior DevOps Engineer** and **System Administrator** experienced in designing, building, and optimizing modern, scalable, and secure infrastructure. I work on modernizing legacy platforms and operating resilient cloud-native and bare-metal production environments.

## Core Expertise & Engineering Domains

- **Security & Code Quality:** Static Application Security Testing (**SAST**) with **SonarQube**, container vulnerability scanning with **Trivy**, secrets management with **HashiCorp Vault**, Software Composition Analysis (**SCA**) & **SBOM** generation, secret scanning, and OS security hardening.
- **Containers & Orchestration:** HA **Kubernetes** cluster provisioning and operations (**Helm**, **RBAC**, **CoreDNS**, **KEDA** autoscaling, **Kubespray**), **Docker**, **Docker Swarm**, and scalable test infrastructure with **Selenium Grid**.
- **Virtualization, Storage & Linux:** On-prem virtualization with **VMware** and **Proxmox**, distributed storage with **Ceph**, **GlusterFS**, **RustFS**, and **MinIO (S3)**, plus **Systemd** and Linux kernel tuning.
- **Observability & Distributed APM:** Full-stack metrics, dashboards, and alerting with **Prometheus**, **Grafana**, and **Alertmanager**; distributed tracing and APM with **OpenTelemetry**, **Jaeger / Zipkin**, **Glowroot**, and **Elastic APM**.
- **Log Management & Compliance:** Regulatory log signing and long-term archiving alongside centralized logging pipelines using **Elasticsearch**, **Kibana**, **Fluent Bit**, and **Filebeat**.
- **CI/CD, IaC & Languages:** Infrastructure as Code with **Terraform** and **Ansible**, automation and tooling in **Python**, **Go**, and **Bash**, plus **GitLab CI** and **Jenkins** pipeline architecture (automated rollback, RBAC, Helm deployments) and **CDN** integrations.
- **Monolithic & Web Services:** Installation, tuning, and production administration of **Apache Tomcat**, **Nginx**, and **Node.js**.
- **Databases, Messaging & Caching:** Cluster setup, optimization, and tuning for **PostgreSQL**, **Supabase**, **Apache Kafka**, **Apache Cassandra**, and **Elasticsearch**, plus distributed caching with **Redis Cluster** and **Memcached**.
