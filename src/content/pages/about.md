I am **Ufkun Karaman**, a **Senior DevOps Engineer** and **System Administrator** experienced in designing, building, and optimizing modern, scalable, and secure infrastructure. I work on modernizing legacy platforms and operating resilient cloud-native and bare-metal production environments.

## Core Expertise & Engineering Domains

- **Security & Code Quality:** Static Code Analysis (SCA) with **SonarQube**, SBOM generation, secret scanning, vulnerability management, dependency tracking, and OS security hardening.
- **Microservices & Kubernetes:** HA Kubernetes cluster provisioning and operations (**Helm**, **RBAC**, **CoreDNS**, **KEDA** autoscaling, **Kubespray**), plus scalable test infrastructure with **Selenium Grid**.
- **Observability & Distributed APM:** Full-stack metrics, dashboards, and alerting with **Prometheus**, **Grafana**, and **Alertmanager**; distributed tracing and APM with **OpenTelemetry**, **Jaeger / Zipkin**, **Glowroot**, and **Elastic APM**.
- **Log Management & Compliance:** Regulatory log signing and long-term archiving alongside centralized logging pipelines using **Elasticsearch**, **Kibana**, **Fluent Bit**, and **Filebeat**.
- **CI/CD & Automation:** End-to-end **GitLab CI** and **Jenkins** pipeline architecture (automated rollback, RBAC, Helm deployments) for both microservices and monolithic workloads, plus **CDN**, **Memcached**, and **GlusterFS** integrations.
- **Monolithic & Web Services:** Installation, tuning, and production administration of **Apache Tomcat**, **Node.js**, and **Nginx**.
- **Databases & Messaging:** Cluster setup, optimization, and tuning for **Apache Cassandra**, **Elasticsearch**, and **Apache Kafka**, as well as **PostgreSQL** system integrations.
- **Distributed Caching:** High-throughput setup and performance optimization for **Redis Cluster** and **Memcached**.
